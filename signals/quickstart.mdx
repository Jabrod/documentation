title: "Quickstart"
description: "From your first upload to the at-risk list."
---

Open [jabrod.com/app/signals](https://jabrod.com/app/signals) and sign in. You have two ways to start:

- **Try it with demo data** creates a workspace from a made-up company's customers, payments, usage and tickets, so you can see a report without your own data.
- **Use your own data** creates an empty workspace. Name it, for example after your company.

A workspace has four steps, in order, and a settings page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Data">
    Upload your customers CSV, and payments, usage events and support tickets if you have them. See [Connect your data](/signals/connect-data).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Columns">
    Check how your columns were matched to Signals' fields, and fix any guess. Then your files are read into the workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Churn">
    Answer **When has a customer left?** and choose how far ahead to predict. Each choice shows how many of your customers it counts as having left. Then choose **Train the model**. See [Churn definition](/signals/churn-definition).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Report">
    Read **How good is this model** first, then the customers at risk, each with their reasons. See [The at-risk report](/signals/weekly-report).
  </Step>
</Steps>

[The model](/signals/model) explains how it is trained, tested and explained.
