---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Start a Signals pilot."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Fill in the form at [jabrod.com/signals/apply](https://jabrod.com/signals/apply).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare your exports">
    Export your customers as CSV. Add payments, usage events and support tickets if you have them. [Connect your data](/signals/connect-data) lists the columns Signals reads.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what churn means">
    Pick one of the three [churn definitions](/signals/churn-definition) for your business.
  </Step>
</Steps>

During a pilot, the Jabrod team loads your exports and trains the model with you. [The model](/signals/model) explains how it is trained, tested and explained.
