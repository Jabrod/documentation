title: "Quickstart"
description: "From your first export to your first weekly at-risk list."
---

<Note>During a pilot, the Jabrod team runs these steps with you. The self-serve workspace, where you do them yourself, isn't open yet.</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Fill in the form at [jabrod.com/signals/apply](https://jabrod.com/signals/apply). We reply to confirm your pilot and its end date. A pilot is free and lasts 4 to 6 weeks; pricing after it is agreed on a call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share exports">
    Export your customers as CSV. Add payments, usage events and support tickets if you have them; each one makes the model better. See [Connect your data](/signals/connect-data).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map columns">
    Each file's columns are matched to Signals' fields. Most are guessed from the column names; you confirm the rest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agree the churn definition">
    Choose what "left" means for your business: cancelled, stopped paying, or went quiet. See [Churn definition](/signals/churn-definition).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Train and check">
    A model is trained on your history and tested on your most recent months. You see how well it did before you see any scores. See [The model](/signals/model).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get the weekly list">
    Every active customer is scored. The [weekly report](/signals/weekly-report) lists those most at risk, with reasons.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When the pilot ends, your data is deleted. See [Data handling](/signals/data-handling).
