The report is titled with its week, for example "Customers at risk, week of 9 Nov 2026".

## The list

Each row is one active customer:

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | Name, or id if there is no name |
| Risk | Chance of churning in the prediction window, as a percentage |
| Rank | Position in the list, highest risk first |
| Reasons | The two or three things raising this customer's risk most |

You can show the top 25, the top 50 or everyone, and search by name or id.

## Model quality

Next to the list, a card shows how well the model did on your most recent months, with a plain verdict. Read it before acting on the list. See [The model](/signals/model#quality-numbers).

## Acting on it

Start at the top. The reasons point to the action: a customer with failed payments needs billing help; one whose usage fell needs a check-in from their account owner.

## Export

Choose **Export CSV** to download the list with every column. Paste it into your CRM or share it with your team.

## Refreshing

During a pilot, the list is rescored when new exports arrive. Automatic weekly refreshes will follow once data can arrive without manual uploads.
