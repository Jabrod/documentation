---
title: "Churn definition"
description: "What counts as a customer leaving."
---

The model learns from customers who left in the past, so "left" has to be defined first. There are three definitions:

| Definition | A customer has churned when | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cancelled** | They have a cancellation date, or a status such as `cancelled` | The customers file |
| **Stopped paying** | No successful payment for 45 days after their last one | A payments file |
| **Went quiet** | No usage events for 30 days | A usage events file |

The number of days for **Stopped paying** and **Went quiet** can be changed.

For **Cancelled**, a customer whose status says they left but who has no cancellation date is counted as leaving on their last activity.

## Prediction window

A score answers: how likely is this customer to churn in the next 30 days?
