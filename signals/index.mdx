<Note>Signals runs as a free pilot with the Jabrod team. [Apply for a pilot](https://jabrod.com/signals/apply). The self-serve workspace isn't open yet.</Note>

Signals does three things:

1. **Connect.** You share exports of your customers, payments, product usage and support tickets.
2. **Predict.** A model trained only on your history scores every active customer's risk of leaving in the next 30 days.
3. **Act.** Each week you get a list of at-risk customers, ranked, with the two or three reasons behind each score.

```text Example row from a weekly report
Customer        Risk   Reasons
Acme Logistics  71%    Usage down 62% in 3 weeks · Two failed payments in 60 days
```

## What Signals is not

- It does not pool your data with anyone else's. Your model is trained on your data alone.
- It does not send customer data to an AI model. Reasons are written from fixed templates using your own numbers.
- It does not hide a weak model. If there is too little history to trust the scores, the report says so.
