<Note>Signals is available at [jabrod.com/app/signals](https://jabrod.com/app/signals). Sign in with your Jabrod account. To work with the Jabrod team on a pilot, [apply here](https://jabrod.com/signals/apply).</Note>

Signals scores each of your active customers on how likely they are to churn in the next 30 days, and gives the reasons behind each score.

1. **Connect.** Upload CSV exports of your customers, and optionally payments, product usage and support tickets.
2. **Predict.** A gradient-boosted model is trained on your history and tested on your most recent weeks.
3. **Act.** Customers are ranked by risk, each with the things raising it most. Export the list as CSV.

```text Example (made-up customer)
Customer        Risk   Reasons
Acme Logistics  71%    Usage down 62% in 3 weeks · Two failed payments in 60 days
```
