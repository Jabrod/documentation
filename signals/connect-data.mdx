Signals reads CSV exports. Only the customers file is required.

| File | Required | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customers | Yes | Who your customers are, when they joined, their plan and revenue, and when they left |
| Payments or subscriptions | Recommended | Failed payments and gaps in paying |
| Usage events | Recommended | How often and how much each customer uses your product |
| Support tickets | Optional | Ticket volume and its trend |

Every file must have a customer id column that matches the customers file.

## Columns

Column names don't have to match these exactly. Signals guesses the mapping from your headers, and you confirm it.

### Customers

| Field | Example | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `customer_id` | `C-1042` | Required |
| `name` | `Acme Logistics` | Shown in the report |
| `signup_at` | `2025-03-14` | Required |
| `plan` | `Growth` | |
| `mrr` | `4999` | Monthly revenue, in your currency |
| `cancelled_at` | `2026-08-02` | Empty while active. Needed for the "Cancelled" churn definition. |

## Dates

Signals detects the date format from each column's values and shows what it found before it imports anything. Use one format per column.

## How much history

Twelve months or more works best. With under 3 months of history, or fewer than about 50 customers who left, the report is marked "not enough history to trust this yet".

## Database access

Read-only access to your database, instead of exports, isn't available yet.
