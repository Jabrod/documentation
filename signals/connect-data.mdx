title: "Connect your data"

---

Signals reads CSV exports, uploaded in a workspace's **Data** step. Only the customers file is required.

| File | Required | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customers | Yes | Who your customers are, when they joined, their plan and revenue, and when they left |
| Payments | No | Failed payments and gaps in paying |
| Usage events | No | How often, and how much, each customer uses your product |
| Support tickets | No | Ticket volume and its trend |

Each file needs a customer id that matches the customers file.

## Columns

Your headers don't have to match these names. Signals guesses the mapping from common header names (for example `account_id`, `joined_on` or `monthly_fee`), and you confirm or fix the guesses in the **Columns** step before the data is used.

## File format

- UTF-8, with or without a byte-order mark. Files that aren't valid UTF-8 are read as Latin-1.
- Rows whose dates can't be read are dropped, and the number dropped is reported.

## How much history

The model is marked "not enough data" with fewer than 50 churned customers, or under 90 days of history.
