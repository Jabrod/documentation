A workspace holds one company's uploads, the model trained on them, and its scores. It belongs to the account that created it.

## Rename

Open the workspace's **Settings**, change **Name**, and choose **Save**.

## Delete on a date

In **Settings**, set **Delete all data on** to a date, for example the day a pilot ends, and choose **Save**. On that date, at 01:00 UTC, the workspace and everything in it is deleted automatically, exactly as below. Clear the date to keep the workspace.

## Delete a workspace now

In **Settings**, type the workspace's name to confirm, then choose **Delete this workspace**. This removes, in one step:

- the uploaded files;
- the customers, payments, usage events and support tickets read from them;
- every trained model, and every score and reason.

A record of the deletion is kept: when it happened and how many rows of each kind were removed. It contains no customer data.

Deleting can't be undone.
