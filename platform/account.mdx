A Jabrod account is an email address and password, or a Google sign-in. The same account works in every Jabrod product.

## Create an account

Go to [jabrod.com/sign-in](https://jabrod.com/sign-in) and choose **Sign up**. You can also start from a product: JBD opens the same page in your browser when you sign in from the app.

## Sign in from the desktop

JBD signs in through your browser, not inside the app.

1. In JBD, choose **Sign in**. Your browser opens jabrod.com.
2. Sign in there. The browser hands JBD a one-time code over `127.0.0.1` on your own computer.
3. JBD exchanges the code for its own session. You can close the browser tab.

JBD never sees your password. Its session is stored in Windows Credential Manager and renews itself; signing out in JBD revokes it.

## What lives where

| Data | Where it is kept |
| --- | --- |
| Your account | Jabrod's account database |
| JBD projects and media | Your computer only |
| AI usage (credits used, by product) | Jabrod's database, so your monthly allowance can be counted |
| Signals, Evals and Context data | Jabrod's database, kept per workspace and owner |

## Delete your account

Email [privacy@jabrod.com](mailto:privacy@jabrod.com) from the address on the account. Self-service deletion isn't available yet.
