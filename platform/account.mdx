A Jabrod account is an email address and password, or a Google sign-in. The same account works in every Jabrod product: JBD, Art, Signals, Evals, Context and their MCP servers.

## Create an account

Go to [jabrod.com/sign-in](https://jabrod.com/sign-in). It takes you to Jabrod's sign-in page on auth.jabrod.com, where you choose **Create account**, or **Continue with Google**. If you forget your password, choose **Forgot password?** to get a reset link by email.

Every product sends you to the same page. See [Sign in with Jabrod](/platform/sign-in) for how this works.

## Sign in from JBD

JBD signs in through your browser, not inside the app.

1. In JBD, choose **Sign in**. Your browser opens Jabrod's sign-in page on auth.jabrod.com.
2. Sign in there. The browser hands JBD a one-time code over `127.0.0.1` on your own computer.
3. JBD exchanges the code for its own session. You can close the browser tab.

JBD never sees your password. Its session is stored in Windows Credential Manager and renews itself; signing out in JBD revokes it.

## Apps and devices

[auth.jabrod.com/account](https://auth.jabrod.com/account) shows the apps and devices signed in to your account. **Sign out everywhere** also signs out JBD on every computer and every MCP app.

## API keys

For MCP clients and CI that can't sign in with Jabrod, make a personal API key at [jabrod.com/app/keys](https://jabrod.com/app/keys).

## What lives where

| Data | Where it is kept |
| --- | --- |
| Your account | Jabrod's account database |
| JBD projects and media | Your computer only |
| AI usage (credits used, by product) | Jabrod's database, so your monthly allowance can be counted |
| Signals, Evals and Context data | Jabrod's database, kept per workspace and owner |

## Delete your account

Email [privacy@jabrod.com](mailto:privacy@jabrod.com) from the address on the account. Self-service deletion isn't available yet.
