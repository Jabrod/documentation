## What the labels mean

- **Live**: anyone can use it now.
- **Pilots**: running with a small number of businesses. Apply on the product page.
- **Taking clients**: offered as a service by the Jabrod team.
- **In development** or **Coming**: being built. Pages describe what it does but list no commands, endpoints or tools until they ship.
