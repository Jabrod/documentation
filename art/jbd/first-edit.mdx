This page takes you from an empty project to an exported video.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a project">
    Choose **File ▸ New Project…** (`Ctrl+N`). JBD saves projects as `.fcproj` files and auto-saves copies to an `Auto-Save` folder next to the project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import media">
    Choose **File ▸ Import Media…**, or open the **Import** mode and choose **Browse files…**. JBD reads MP4 and MOV video; WAV, MP3, FLAC, AIFF and Ogg audio; and PNG and JPEG stills.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put clips on the timeline">
    Drag a clip from the media panel onto the timeline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cut and trim">
    Press `C` for the Razor Tool and click a clip to split it. Press `V` for the Selection Tool, then drag a clip's edge to trim it. `Ctrl+Z` undoes any edit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export">
    Choose **File ▸ Export…** (`Ctrl+M`) or the **Export** button in the title bar, pick a preset, and choose **Export**. On JBD Free, exports are up to 720p with a small "Made with JBD" mark. [JBD Pro](/art/jbd/pro-and-credits) removes the mark and exports at 1080p and 4K.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Try it without your own footage

The **Import** mode has three starting points:

| Card | What it adds |
| --- | --- |
| Demo footage | Six generated 1080p clips with sound |
| Demo project | A cut sequence with transitions, effects and music |
| Bars and Tone | SMPTE HD bars with a 1 kHz reference tone |
