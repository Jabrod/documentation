## Free and Pro

| | JBD Free | JBD Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Editing, colour, audio, captions | Yes | Yes |
| Export | Up to 720p, with a "Made with JBD" mark | 1080p and 4K, no mark |
| AI Director | No | Yes |
| AI credits each month | 0 | 1,000 |
| Pay-as-you-go | No | Optional |

Buy JBD Pro from inside JBD, or at [jabrod.com/jbd/upgrade](https://jabrod.com/jbd/upgrade).

## Art Pro

Art Pro will replace JBD Pro when the Art studio launches. It includes the Art studio, the AI Director in JBD and monthly AI credits, monthly or yearly. It is not on sale yet. Prices are on [jabrod.com/pricing](https://jabrod.com/pricing).

## AI credits

**1 credit is $0.01 of what the AI provider charges Jabrod.** A short planning step costs a few credits; a generated video costs more. The Director shows a video's cost before you approve it.

- The allowance resets on the first day of each calendar month.
- A video that fails to generate is refunded automatically.
- When credits run out, the Director stops until the next month, unless pay-as-you-go is on.

## Pay-as-you-go

Pay-as-you-go lets a Pro member keep using the Director after the monthly credits are spent.

1. In JBD, open **Settings ▸ Usage** and choose **Turn on pay-as-you-go**.
2. Your browser opens jabrod.com. Set up payment by UPI AutoPay or card.
3. Credits used past the allowance are added up and charged once, at the end of each billing cycle.

You set a monthly spending limit. When it is reached, the Director stops until the next cycle. You can pause pay-as-you-go at any time from the same place.

## See your usage

**Settings ▸ Usage** shows what the Director used over the last 7, 30 or 90 days, by kind (thinking, video, voiceover) and by day, as a chart or a table.
