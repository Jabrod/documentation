## Free and Art Pro

| | Free | Art Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Art Studio: chat, Image and Voice Studios, boards, Library, Editor | 50 AI credits a month | The full studio, with the credits below |
| Art Studio for Windows (JBD): editing, colour, audio, captions | Yes | Yes |
| JBD exports | Up to 720p, with a "Made with JBD" mark | 1080p and 4K, no mark |
| AI Director in Art Studio for Windows | No | Yes |
| AI credits each month | 50, in Art Studio | 1,000 |
| Pay-as-you-go | No | Optional |

Get Art Pro at [jabrod.com/art](https://jabrod.com/art#plans), or from **Plans** inside Art Studio. Prices are shown there, monthly or yearly. Art Pro replaced JBD Pro.

## AI credits

**1 credit is $0.01 of what the AI provider charges Jabrod.** A short planning step costs a few credits, and each studio shows a generation's cost before you run it.

- The allowance resets on the first day of each calendar month.
- When credits run out, generation and the Director stop until the next month, unless pay-as-you-go is on.

## Pay-as-you-go

Pay-as-you-go lets an Art Pro member keep using the Director after the monthly credits are spent.

1. In Art Studio for Windows (JBD), open **Settings ▸ Usage** and choose **Turn on pay-as-you-go**.
2. Your browser opens jabrod.com. Set up payment by UPI AutoPay or card.
3. Credits used past the allowance are added up and charged once, at the end of each billing cycle.

You set a monthly spending limit. When it is reached, the Director stops until the next cycle. You can pause pay-as-you-go at any time from the same place.

## See your usage

In Art Studio for Windows, **Settings ▸ Usage** shows what the Director used over the last 7, 30 or 90 days, by kind and by day, as a chart or a table.
