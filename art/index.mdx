## JBD in one paragraph

JBD has a multi-track timeline with ripple, roll, slip and slide trims; colour grading with curves, wheels, looks and scopes; audio mixing with loudness meters; titles and editable caption tracks with on-device speech to text; and export up to 4K. The AI Director takes a request in plain words, plans the edit, works on your timeline step by step, and checks the result on rendered frames. Every change it makes can be undone.

JBD is free. [JBD Pro](/art/jbd/pro-and-credits) adds the Director, higher export resolutions and no watermark.
