Art Studio is Jabrod's creative app, at [art.jabrod.com/studio](https://art.jabrod.com/studio). Sign in with your [Jabrod account](/platform/account) and everything is in one place:

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Chat** | A general assistant that can write generation prompts for you |
| **Image Studio** and **Voice Studio** | Generate images and voice lines, with each one's credit cost shown first |
| **Canvas** | Boards where you arrange prompts, media and notes, and wire them together |
| **Library** | Every file you've uploaded or generated |
| **Editor** | The JBD video editor, in your browser |

Video generation isn't available yet. Upload clips instead: they work on boards, in the Library and in the Editor.

## Plans

Every account gets 50 AI credits free each month. [Art Pro](/art/jbd/pro-and-credits) adds the full studio, the AI Director in Art Studio for Windows, and 1,000 AI credits a month. Prices are on [jabrod.com/art](https://jabrod.com/art#plans), or **Plans** inside Art Studio.
