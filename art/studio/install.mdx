## In your browser

Go to [art.jabrod.com/studio](https://art.jabrod.com/studio) and choose **Sign in to Jabrod**. You sign in on auth.jabrod.com with your [Jabrod account](/platform/account), and come back to Art Studio.

## Install it as an app

Art Studio can be installed from the browser, so it opens in its own window, like any other app.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Chrome and Edge, Android">
    Open Art Studio and choose **Install Studio** in the sidebar, then confirm. The button appears when your browser can install it.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="iPhone and iPad">
    Safari has no install button, so **Install Studio** shows the steps:

    1. Tap **Share** in Safari's toolbar.
    2. Choose **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The installed app opens from your home screen or app list. It needs an internet connection: without one, it shows a page saying so. Once Art Studio is running as an installed app, the **Install Studio** button goes away.

## On Windows

[Art Studio for Windows (JBD)](/art/jbd/install) is a separate desktop editor with the AI Director.
