---
title: "The Editor"
description: "The JBD video editor in your browser, with your Art Studio files."
---

**Editor** in Art Studio's sidebar opens the JBD video editor in your browser. It is the same editor as [Art Studio for Windows (JBD)](/art/jbd/install), built for the web.

## Open files

- **From the Library:** in the Editor, choose **Open from your Library** and pick a file. Or choose **Open in Editor** on a file in the Library.
- **From a board:** in an **Export** node, choose **Edit in the Editor** to open the media wired into it.

## Save your edit

1. In the editor, choose **File**, then **Export**.
2. Choose **Save an export to your Library**, and pick the export.

The file is saved to your Library, where you can download it or use it on a board.

## Good to know

- Editing works best on a computer. On a phone the editor is cramped and slower.
- The web editor runs on one thread, so long or high-resolution exports take a while.
- The AI Director isn't in the web Editor yet. It is in [Art Studio for Windows](/art/jbd/director).
