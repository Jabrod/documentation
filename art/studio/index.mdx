<Note>The Art studio isn't released yet. This page describes what it will do. Setup steps will be added when you can use it.</Note>

The Art studio is a canvas at art.jabrod.com/studio. You put nodes on a board and wire them together: an idea, the images and clips generated from it, a voice line, and an export. Video edits go on to [JBD](/art/jbd/install).

## Nodes

| Node | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Prompt | The idea, as text. Connect it to a generator to fill that generator's prompt. |
| Image | An image generated from a prompt, or uploaded. |
| Video | A clip generated from a prompt, or uploaded. |
| Audio | A voice made from text, or an uploaded audio file. |
| Note | Plain text for annotating the board. |
| Export | Collects the media wired into it, for download and for JBD. |

## Boards

Boards save automatically and come back exactly as you left them: nodes, connections, media and zoom.

## Credits

Every signed-in account gets a free monthly allowance for generation in the studio. Art Pro includes the studio with more monthly AI credits; see [Plans](/platform/pricing). Failed generations are not charged.

## Open source

The studio will be published under Apache-2.0. Accounts, generation and credits stay on jabrod.com. Run without a Jabrod backend, the studio keeps boards in your browser and turns generation off.
