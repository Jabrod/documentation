<Note>The Art studio isn't released yet. Setup and usage will be documented here when you can use it.</Note>

The Art studio is a canvas where you generate images, video clips and voice lines, arrange them on boards, and take the video into [JBD](/art/jbd/install) to edit.

Plans for the Art studio are on [jabrod.com/art](https://jabrod.com/art#plans).
