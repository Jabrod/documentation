Jabrod Evals tests retrieval systems, agents and support assistants. You give it:

- a **test set**: questions your users really ask, with what a good answer looks like;
- a **target**: the AI application to test, reached over HTTP, or a [Context](/context/index) workspace.

Evals sends every question to the target, scores every answer with the **suites** you pick, and gives you a **report**: pass rates by suite, each failure with its reason, and the change since the last run.

## Suites

| Suite | Checks that |
| --- | --- |
| Grounded in sources | Every claim in the answer is supported by the passages the app retrieved |
| Correctness | The answer agrees with the expected answer and contains the required phrases |
| Correct refusals | The app declines out-of-scope questions, and only those |
| Tool call arguments | The app calls the expected tool with the expected arguments |
| Policy and tone | The answer follows a policy you write |
