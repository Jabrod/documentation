# Introduction

Welcome to **Jabrod** - the RAG as a Service platform. Build AI-powered applications with knowledge bases in minutes.

## What is Jabrod?

Jabrod is a modular AI platform for developers and teams building real-world AI systems. Start with Retrieval-Augmented Generation today, and scale into agents, workflows, and model context tooling — without changing platforms.
