---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Create a workspace, add sources and ask a question."
---

<Note>Context isn't open to the public yet.</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Use your [Jabrod account](/platform/account).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a workspace">
    Give it a name, such as `Support policies`. Put sources that answer the same kind of question in one workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add sources">
    Upload up to 10 files at a time (PDF, DOCX, Markdown or TXT, 10 MB each), or paste a web page's URL. Each source shows its progress: queued, parsing, embedding, ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask">
    Type a question. The answer cites its sources with numbers; select a number to see the exact passage, its document and where in the document it is.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check it with Evals

Every question asked in a workspace is kept, so you can turn real questions into a test set and score the workspace with [Jabrod Evals](/evals/targets#context-workspace).
