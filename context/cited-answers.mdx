## Retrieval

A question is searched two ways inside its workspace:

- **By meaning**: the question's embedding against every passage's embedding.
- **By keyword**: the question's words of three letters or more against the passages' full text. One rare word is enough to match.

The two rankings are merged with reciprocal-rank fusion: 24 candidates in, the top 6 passages out. A passage that ranks well in both rises to the top.

## The answer

The model gets the question and the six passages, numbered, and is told to:

- answer only from the passages;
- end every sentence that states a fact with the number of the passage that supports it, like `[1]` or `[2][3]`;
- say exactly "I can't find this in your sources." when the passages don't answer the question.

## Citations

The numbers in the answer are renumbered 1, 2, 3 in reading order. Each citation carries:

| Field | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Number | `1` |
| Document | `Returns policy.pdf` |
| Locator | `Page 2` |
| Passage | The exact text the claim rests on |
| Source URL | For web pages |

## Refusals

A refusal is an answer, not an error. It means the workspace has nothing on the question. Add a source that covers it, or check that the source finished ingesting.

To check how often Context refuses correctly, and how often its answers are grounded, score the workspace with [Evals](/evals/suites).

## Models

Answers are written by open-weight models. If one is busy, the next one in the list answers.
